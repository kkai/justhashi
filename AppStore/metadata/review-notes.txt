No account, no login, no demo credentials needed. Everything works offline and
all progress is stored on the device.

There is one non-consumable in-app purchase, de.kaikunze.hashi.full, which
unlocks six of the nine lessons, the practice drills, the escalating hints,
large boards and the stats screen. Free play covers the rules lesson plus the
first two techniques, small and medium boards at every difficulty, the daily
puzzle, and the error check.

Two things that may look inconsistent but are deliberate:

1. Large boards are behind the purchase, yet the daily puzzle serves a large
   board on Saturdays and Sundays and it is free to play. That is intentional.
   The daily is exempt from the size gate so that free players get a taste of
   the larger boards once a week; solving one offers the unlock afterwards.

2. Tapping Large in the size picker bounces the selection back and opens the
   purchase screen instead of disabling the segment, so the feature stays
   visible rather than hidden.

Restore purchases is in Settings and is always reachable, including when the
app already considers itself unlocked. Restoring on an account that owns
nothing reports "Nothing to restore on this Apple Account" rather than failing
silently.

The app collects no data of any kind. The privacy manifest declares only
UserDefaults under reason CA92.1, which matches the "Data Not Collected"
answers in App Privacy.
